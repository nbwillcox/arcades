HOW TO PLAY

Ride your route Monday to Sunday. Glowing yellow houses are subscribers: land a paper on the welcome mat (250), the patio (150) or in the mailbox (100). The grumpy dark houses are not customers — smash their windows (150)!

Break a subscriber's window, or miss their house, and they cancel (-250). Lose every subscriber and you are fired.

Dodge hover-cars, cross traffic, skaters, black holes, UFO beams and meteors. Hop over low things. Grab paper bundles to restock.

Power-ups: S super speed · I invincible · ∞ infinite papers.

Chain hits for a combo multiplier. After each route: the Hover Academy bonus course. Sunday ends with a boss.
